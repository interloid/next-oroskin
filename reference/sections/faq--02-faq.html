      Order & Shipping

      Delivery windows, tracking, packaging

      How long does delivery take?

      Complimentary express delivery arrives in 2–3 business days worldwide. Each parcel ships from the nearest atelier — Paris, New York, or Seoul — so nothing crosses more borders than it has to.

      Where is my order right now?

      Open your account and choose Orders: every parcel has its live tracking link there. You also receive it by email the moment your order leaves the atelier.

      Do you ship internationally?

      Yes, to more than 60 countries. Duties and taxes are shown at checkout, so there is nothing more to pay on delivery.

      How is my order packaged?

      In recycled card and paper only, without plastic fillers. Refills travel in their own sleeve, ready to slip into your case.

      My Account

      Sign in, profile, saved details

      How do I create an account?

      Choose Login in the top navigation and switch to Create Account. You only need a name, an email and a password — we never ask for anything we do not use.

      I have forgotten my password.

      Choose Login, then Forgot your password, and enter your email. We send you a link to choose a new one; it stays valid for 24 hours.

      Can I change my saved address?

      Yes. In your account, open Addresses to edit, add or remove one, and choose which is your default for checkout.

      How do I close my account?

      Write to us from the contact page with the email of your account. We close it within two working days and confirm once your data is deleted.

      Returns & Exchanges

      Sixty days, collected from your door

      What is your return policy?

      You have sixty days to return any unopened product, and thirty days for opened skincare that did not suit you. Returns are always free.

      How do I start a return?

      Open the order in your account and choose Return items, or write to us. We book a courier to collect the parcel from your door.

      Can I exchange a shade or size?

      Yes. Choose Exchange instead of Return and pick the new shade or size; we send it as soon as the courier has collected the first one.

      When will I get my refund?

      Within five working days after the parcel reaches us, back to the card or account you paid with.

      Payment & BillingCards, invoices, promo codes 4 answers

      Payment & Billing

      Cards, invoices, promo codes

      Which payment methods do you accept?

      All major cards, Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal. The methods available in your country are shown at checkout.

      Is my payment information secure?

      Yes. Payments are handled by Shopify on encrypted connections, and we never see or store your full card number.

      Can I get an invoice for my order?

      Your order confirmation email is your receipt. For a company invoice with your tax number, write to us with the order number.

      How do I use a promo code?

      Enter it in the Discount code field at checkout. One code applies per order, and the saving shows before you pay.

      Products & Ingredients

      Formulas, suitability, results

      Are your formulas suitable for sensitive skin?

      Every formula is dermatologist-tested, fragrance-optional and free of drying alcohols. The Frost and Pearl lines are built specifically for reactive skin.

      Is the gold in your formulas real?

      Yes. Our gold-infused formulas use pure 24-carat gold leaf, suspended in small amounts for radiance; it is safe on skin and never coloured.

      How long until I see results?

      Most clients see a difference in texture within two weeks, and in tone and firmness after six to eight weeks of daily use.

      Do you test on animals?

      Never. OroSkin is cruelty-free and vegan, and none of our ingredient suppliers test on animals either.
