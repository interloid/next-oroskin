Chapter I · Fragrance

    Rose picked before the heat can take its oil.

    Damask rose loses its most volatile molecules the moment the sun warms the petal, so our growers in Isparta harvest between 4 and 8am and the flowers reach the still the same morning. It takes roughly 3,600 blossoms to yield the rose absolute in one 30ml eau de parfum, dosed at 22% concentration, naturals-led, and verified for six hours of wear on skin rather than on blotter paper.

    3,600 Blossoms per 30ml flacon

    Chapter II · Skincare

    Actives kept at their clinical dose, not their marketing dose.

    Every serum starts from the concentration the studies used, not the one that looks good on a label. We print each active's percentage on the carton, test every batch for stability at three temperatures, and keep reformulating until the texture is as good as the dose: often more than forty rounds before a formula is signed off.

    40+ Formulation rounds per serum

    Chapter III · Makeup

    Forty-two shades mapped by undertone, not lightness.

    Our colour lab matched the complexion range across warm, neutral, olive and cool undertone families, then re-tested every shade under daylight, tungsten and evening LED so it reads true in all three. Lip and cheek pigments are pressed with camellia and squalane so colour wears like skincare, and every powder pan and lipstick bullet drops into a refillable case.

    42 Shades, four undertone families

    Chapter IV · The ritual

    Finally, it becomes three quiet minutes.

    The last brief we write is emotional: how the flacon should sound when opened, how the cream should feel at the third use, what the scent should leave behind on a linen sleeve. Fragrance, skincare and colour arrive in glass and aluminium made to be refilled: 71% less carbon per use, and no virgin plastic anywhere in the line.
