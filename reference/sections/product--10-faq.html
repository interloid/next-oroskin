FAQ

A short conversation.

Can I use this with retinol?

Yes — alternate nights to begin. After a month most skins tolerate both in one routine, serum first.

Will it break me out?

It's non-comedogenic and fragrance-free. Patch test first; if your skin feels tight, use it every other day for the first week.

Is it pregnancy safe?

It contains no retinoids or salicylic acid, but please check with your doctor before starting new skincare.

How long does one bottle last?

About two months, using two or three drops morning and night.

Is it suitable for sensitive skin?

Yes. It's dermatologist tested on sensitive skin, with no alcohol or added fragrance.
