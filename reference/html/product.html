    Highlights

    Six reasons it earns its shelf.

    Ritual Serum No. 01 was formulated backwards — from how skin feels at the end of the day, to the molecules that get it there.

    • 72-hour hydration

      A lipid-mimicking veil of ceramide NP, squalane and glacial water holds moisture through three full days.

    • Small-batch craft

      Blended in Grasse in batches of 400, cold-filled into recyclable glass and numbered by hand.

    • Soft-focus finish

    How to use

    Four minutes, performed the same way, every evening.

    1. Begin on skin still faintly damp — the serum travels further on moisture.

    2. Warm four drops between your palms for a few seconds.

    3. Press your palms to the cheeks, forehead and neck, holding each for a breath.

    4. Finish with your moisturiser and let it settle before bed.

    FAQ

    A short conversation.

    Can I use this with retinol?

    Yes — alternate nights to begin. After a month most skins tolerate both in one routine, serum first.

    Will it break me out?

    It's non-comedogenic and fragrance-free. Patch test first; if your skin feels tight, use it every other day for the first week.

    Is it pregnancy safe?

    It contains no retinoids or salicylic acid, but please check with your doctor before starting new skincare.

    How long does one bottle last?

    About two months, using two or three drops morning and night.

    Is it suitable for sensitive skin?

    Yes. It's dermatologist tested on sensitive skin, with no alcohol or added fragrance.
